💻 PAPADI PARK POCKET CLASS

Learning support that fits right in your pocket.

Papadi Park Pocket Class brings the learning support children need closer to home — through the devices already in their hands.

Pocket Class provides online homework help, tutoring and learning support, connecting children with a Papadi Park tutor through a simple digital experience. Whether a child needs help understanding a difficult concept, working through homework, preparing for a test or simply gaining more confidence in a subject, Pocket Class gives them an opportunity to learn with guidance and encouragement.

A classroom without walls

The name “Pocket Class” reflects the way we learn today: the classroom no longer has to be a physical place. With a phone, tablet or computer, meaningful learning can happen wherever the child is.

Just like Papadi Park’s other educational services, Pocket Class is built around our belief that children learn best when learning feels approachable, engaging and supportive.

🚀

From individual homework assistance to tutoring and future learning programmes, Papadi Park Pocket Class is designed to grow alongside the needs of children and families.
