Our Story & Mission

21 Chunks is a supplementary Edutainment programme providing Play-centred learning activities, Broadcasted educational programmes and Tutoring services.

Our drive is to assist the wholistic development of Children. We propel the process of building the next generation of Greats by providing fun “Learn through Play” programmes.

All content and activities are fully based on your preferred Curriculum/Programme.
